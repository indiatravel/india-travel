Two streets, a whole afternoon

Park Street is a starting point, not a to-do list. Follow the shopfronts, stop for a bite, and notice the details above eye level.

Read the street

Look for signs, balconies and the rhythm of old buildings. Leave yourself time to sit down; an afternoon in a city need not be measured in kilometres.

Order with curiosity

Choose a busy place, ask what is freshly made, and mention any dietary needs clearly. A short food stop is a good reason to linger rather than another task to finish.

One practical note

Keep belongings close in busy areas and use safe crossings. Treat the archival-looking yellow-taxi photograph as a scene, not a current transport promise.
