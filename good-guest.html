TRAVEL GENTLY

A good guest
leaves room for others.

Four small habits that make an India journey more considerate. Nothing complicated — just a little care.

01 / A SMALL HABIT

Ask, don’t assume

A portrait, a doorway, a working tea field: permission matters. A friendly question is better than a hurried photograph.

02 / A SMALL HABIT

Buy a little more locally

A locally run meal or thoughtfully chosen craft keeps a visit connected to the people who live there. Agree prices clearly and pay fairly.

03 / A SMALL HABIT

Carry out what you carry in

Take a refillable bottle where safe refills are available. Keep a small rubbish bag and leave natural souvenirs where you found them.

04 / A SMALL HABIT

Let conditions change your plan

Heat, rough seas, rain and fatigue are good reasons to do less. Follow posted guidance and local advice instead of trying to complete a checklist.

Put a gentler pace into practice.
