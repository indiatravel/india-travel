Swaraj Dweep · Andaman Islands

A shore worth slowing down for

At Kala Pathar, let a beach walk be enough. Sand, shade and the changing colour of the water need little embellishment.

Enjoy the edge of the sea

Walk the accessible shore, seek shade and take breaks. A beautiful beach is not automatically a safe place to swim; follow local advice and posted warnings.

Keep the coast intact

Leave shells and coral where they belong. Carry your rubbish out and do not disturb wildlife for a closer photograph.

One practical note

Plan island transfers with a weather buffer. Check current local conditions before setting out and avoid isolated shoreline areas after dark.
