OUR APPROACH

A journal, not
a checklist.

India Travel Blog is an independent collection of short travel ideas. We care more about a thoughtful day than a long list of stops.

Small stories. Real places.

These are compact editorial suggestions, not personal trip reports or promises about current conditions. Check access, transport operations, weather and local guidance before setting out.

Honest by design.

There are no invented testimonials, public ratings or popularity counters. The photographs are real, openly reusable images; their creators and licenses are linked in our photography register.

Your device, your notes.

Saved places, ratings, reactions, poll choices and private notes use your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to the journal, shared with other readers, or synced between devices. Clearing browser data removes them.

The newsletter enquiry prepares an email draft. It does not subscribe you or store your email. If hosted on a platform with separate analytics, that host may collect technical usage data under its own privacy policy.
