Make the ride the plan

Choose a service only after checking the current timetable and booking information. Leave room around departure time instead of stacking the day with stops.

Watch the close-up world

The railway brings the hillside close: bends, gardens and neighbourhoods unfold slowly. Stay inside permitted areas and photograph from a safe position.

One practical note

Carry a layer for cool weather and check current operations with the railway. Never walk onto active tracks for a photograph.
